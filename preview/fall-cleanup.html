Questions

Before you call.

Anything not answered here, text us. You will hear back from us, not a call center.

Why two visits instead of one?

Leaves drop from October through November. One early visit leaves you with a full yard by Thanksgiving. Two visits, each timed to a city pickup, means your lawn goes into winter clean.

Do you bag the leaves?

No. Everything goes to the curb, and Livonia's bulk leaf pickup hauls it for free. That is part of why the price is what it is.

Is gutter cleaning really free?

Yes. Gutters are cleared on your final visit and are included in every Full Fall Cleanup. There is no separate charge.

Can I book just one visit?

Yes. One visit is $415 in Rosedale Gardens and $575 in North Livonia. The season is only $20 more and includes the gutters.

When exactly will you come?

Your weeks are set as soon as the city publishes its pickup schedule for your section. Each visit lands 5 to 7 days before your pickup.
