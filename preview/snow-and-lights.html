Questions

Before you call.

Anything not answered here, text us. You will hear back from us, not a call center.

What if it barely snows?

You are paying for a crew that is ready for every storm from November 1 to March 15. The season price is what makes that possible, in a light winter or a heavy one.

What if it snows all winter?

Your total can never go past the maximum in the table, no matter how many storms come. That number is the promise.

When will you clear my driveway?

Within 24 hours of the storm ending, and never before 7 a.m. You get a time-stamped photo after every visit.

Do I have to buy my own lights?

Yes. You pick the lights you like and they stay yours. We install them, supply the timer, take them down in January, and reinstall them next year for $75 less.

Can I get lights and a fall cleanup together?

Yes. The Showcase package includes a full-season Fall Cleanup with your lights.
