Our story

Livonia is home.

Mangiapane Brothers started the way most family businesses do: a few brothers, a mower, and neighbors who needed the work done right. Livonia is home. The houses we service are the houses we grew up around.

We provide full-service property maintenance for those who take pride in their home. Lawn, snow, cleanups, gutters, and landscaping, all from one company that answers its own phone.

Every crew is trained and managed by us, and every job is held to the standard we would want on our own homes. When we say we will be there, we are there.
