Before you call.

Anything not answered here, call us. You will reach us, not a call center.

What does a cut cost?

Most lawns start at $55 a cut for weekly service. Larger lots are priced on the call, and you get the number in writing before the first cut.

Weekly or biweekly?

Weekly keeps the lawn at its best through the growing season. Biweekly works on many lawns, and we will tell you honestly if yours needs weekly.

What happens when it rains?

If rain cancels your week, it is skipped and noted on your invoice. You are never billed for a cut that did not happen.

Where do you work?

Livonia and Westland, with routes in Garden City, Canton, Dearborn, and Dearborn Heights. Call with your address and we will tell you on the spot.

How do I pay?

One invoice a month. Checks are payable to Mangiapane Brothers LLC.
