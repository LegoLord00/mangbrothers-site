# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in Livonia and Westland, Michigan (also Garden City, Canton, Dearborn, Dearborn Heights) deciding who to hire for the current season's work: weekly mowing, a fall leaf cleanup, Christmas lights, or seasonal snow removal. Many are established, older homeowners who take pride in their property. Most arrive on a phone, often from a door hanger.
- **Secondary:** lenders, investors, and advisors evaluating the business. The same site must read as a serious, professional operation to them.

## Product Purpose
The public website for Mangiapane Brothers Lawn and Snow LLC (mangbrothers.com). It builds credibility and gets the visitor to call (734) 450-2400. Success is a phone call; nothing on the site converts on its own.

## Positioning
Not the cheapest, the most dependable. One local crew, one phone number, every season of the year (lawn, fall cleanup, lights, snow), with starting prices published and every quote given in writing before work starts.

## Operating Context
- Seasons drive everything: spring/summer lawn and landscaping, fall cleanup timed to Livonia's bulk leaf pickup, Christmas lights and snow removal November through March.
- Physical marketing (door hangers) sends people to the site; "how did you hear about us" is asked on the phone.
- Hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages; launch October 22, 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **The phone call is the only action.** No forms, no booking flow, no texting option. Click-to-call and email only.
- **Starting prices stay on the site** for every standardized service.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no CMS, no build step.
- Undecided: analytics on call/email taps (Google Analytics pending an account).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Mangiapane Brothers Lawn and Snow LLC. Tagline, used verbatim or not at all: "Reliable Service. Uncompromising Standards."
- Existing oak-tree logo (preview/img/logo.png, logo-white.png) and brand green #1B4D2E.
- Copy reads like a trusted neighbor who runs a professional operation: plain, direct, no corporate jargon.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real photo of the three brothers (preview/img/brothers.jpg).
- Four real before/after job photo pairs (preview/img/work/); originals without baked-in labels still to be found.
- Published prices from the 2026 price sheets (lawn from $55 a cut; fall cleanup $435/$595; snow $550/$590 with capped totals; lights from $625).
- Founded 2019, based in Livonia, fully insured.
- **No customer reviews or testimonials exist yet.** Do not invent reviews, ratings, customer counts, or awards.
- No fall or snow job photos yet.

## Product Principles
1. Every screen points to the phone number.
2. Show prices; never hide behind "call for a quote" where a starting price exists.
3. Dependability is proven with specifics (dates, caps, promises), not adjectives.
4. The seasons are the structure: the visitor should always know which season's service they are looking at.
